{%- comment -%} Mimi, come to sweep up. Drawn after the illustration Qianjun supplied rather than after a photograph: soft ginger fur shaded with gradients, a round head wider at the cheeks than at the crown, and eyes large enough to fill their sockets — which, with the highlights, is most of what separates a drawing that reads as cute from one that reads as a diagram of a cat. She keeps her own ginger ramp instead of the page tokens: an orange tabby needs more than one orange, and flattening her to the accent is what made the first attempt look like a pictogram. The ramp is warm and sits beside the Butter palette without clashing. Parts are named for the animation — tail, head, each paw, and the broom, which turns about the paw holding it. drop.js reads `.cat-bristles` for the point the sweep lands on. {%- endcomment -%}
