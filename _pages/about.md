I am a Master's student in Mechanical Engineering (Research Track) at **Columbia University**,
concentrating on robotics and control. In the
[Creative Machines Lab](https://www.creativemachineslab.com/), advised by
[Jiong Lin](https://jl6017.github.io/) and [Hod Lipson](https://www.hodlipson.com/),
I work on **video-based parameter estimation** for deformable objects and soft robots,
and on **generative models for mechanism design**.

Before Columbia I earned my Bachelor's degree at **Shanghai Jiao Tong University**,
advised by [Prof. Dong Wang](https://me.sjtu.edu.cn/en/FullTimeTeacher/wangdong1.html),
where I built magnetic wire-guiding robots for minimally invasive surgery — mechanical
design, control, and elastic-rod modeling.

My broader interests lie in **robot learning**, **physics-based simulation** and
**3D computer vision**.
